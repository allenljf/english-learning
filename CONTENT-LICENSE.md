# Content sources and licenses

The 1,861 supplemental English headwords were selected in frequency order from the English data distributed with [wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq). Wordfreq credits Robyn Speer and includes data from Google Books Ngrams, the Leeds Internet Corpus, Wikipedia, ParaCrawl, OPUS/OpenSubtitles, and the SUBTLEX word lists by Marc Brysbaert and colleagues. The included word-frequency data is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The supplemental English-to-Chinese meanings are adapted from [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), released under the [MIT License](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT/blob/master/LICENSE). Chinese entries were converted to Taiwan Traditional Chinese. The MIT notice is preserved in `licenses/ECDICT-LICENSE.txt`. Example sentences were written for this learning site.

The English spelling list was checked against the [Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict); no CMUdict pronunciations are included in this project. Carnegie Mellon University requests acknowledgement when the dictionary is used.

The original project vocabulary and original practice material are separate contributions. New passages, questions, tense scenarios, and listening scripts were written for this project; they are not official TOEIC or company test materials.
