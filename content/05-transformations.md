# 時態 × 語態 × 肯定否定疑問：72 格變化實驗室

同一個動作 check，變更時間與觀察角度。每組中文描述的是肯定句情境；否定句否定該敘述，疑問句詢問該敘述是否成立。主動主詞固定為 the technician，受詞 the sensor；被動主詞改為 the sensor。p.p. 是過去分詞，不是過去式的同義詞。

優先精熟現在／過去簡單、現在進行、現在完成及 will 未來；再補其他組合。標有「辨識用」的被動形式非常少見，不建議當成日常表達模板。

## 現在簡單式

情境：習慣、事實；每天例行檢查。

主動公式：`V / V-s`；被動公式：`am/is/are + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician checks the sensor every day. | The technician does not check the sensor every day. | Does the technician check the sensor every day? |
| 被動 | The sensor is checked every day. | The sensor is not checked every day. | Is the sensor checked every day? |

## 過去簡單式

情境：已結束的過去；昨天檢查。

主動公式：`V-ed / 不規則過去`；被動公式：`was/were + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician checked the sensor yesterday. | The technician did not check the sensor yesterday. | Did the technician check the sensor yesterday? |
| 被動 | The sensor was checked yesterday. | The sensor was not checked yesterday. | Was the sensor checked yesterday? |

## 未來簡單式

情境：預測、承諾；明天會檢查。

主動公式：`will + V`；被動公式：`will be + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician will check the sensor tomorrow. | The technician will not check the sensor tomorrow. | Will the technician check the sensor tomorrow? |
| 被動 | The sensor will be checked tomorrow. | The sensor will not be checked tomorrow. | Will the sensor be checked tomorrow? |

## 現在進行式

情境：現在進行中的動作；正在檢查。

主動公式：`am/is/are + V-ing`；被動公式：`am/is/are being + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician is checking the sensor now. | The technician is not checking the sensor now. | Is the technician checking the sensor now? |
| 被動 | The sensor is being checked now. | The sensor is not being checked now. | Is the sensor being checked now? |

## 過去進行式

情境：過去某時正在做；昨天十點正在檢查。

主動公式：`was/were + V-ing`；被動公式：`was/were being + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician was checking the sensor at ten yesterday. | The technician was not checking the sensor at ten yesterday. | Was the technician checking the sensor at ten yesterday? |
| 被動 | The sensor was being checked at ten yesterday. | The sensor was not being checked at ten yesterday. | Was the sensor being checked at ten yesterday? |

## 未來進行式

情境：未來某時正在做；明天十點會正在檢查。

主動公式：`will be + V-ing`；被動公式：`will be being + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician will be checking the sensor at ten tomorrow. | The technician will not be checking the sensor at ten tomorrow. | Will the technician be checking the sensor at ten tomorrow? |
| 被動（辨識用） | The sensor will be being checked at ten tomorrow. | The sensor will not be being checked at ten tomorrow. | Will the sensor be being checked at ten tomorrow? |

此被動在形式上可組合，但冗長且少見。實際表達優先使用本列主動句。若不重要的是持續過程，可改用簡單或完成被動，但語意重點會改變。

## 現在完成式

情境：過去到現在的完成結果；已檢查過，結果與現在有關。

主動公式：`have/has + p.p.`；被動公式：`have/has been + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician has checked the sensor. | The technician has not checked the sensor yet. | Has the technician checked the sensor yet? |
| 被動 | The sensor has been checked. | The sensor has not been checked yet. | Has the sensor been checked yet? |

## 過去完成式

情境：在另一過去事件以前已完成；主管抵達前已檢查。

主動公式：`had + p.p.`；被動公式：`had been + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician had checked the sensor before the supervisor arrived. | The technician had not checked the sensor before the supervisor arrived. | Had the technician checked the sensor before the supervisor arrived? |
| 被動 | The sensor had been checked before the supervisor arrived. | The sensor had not been checked before the supervisor arrived. | Had the sensor been checked before the supervisor arrived? |

## 未來完成式

情境：到未來參考點已完成；到明天中午會已檢查。

主動公式：`will have + p.p.`；被動公式：`will have been + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician will have checked the sensor by noon tomorrow. | The technician will not have checked the sensor by noon tomorrow. | Will the technician have checked the sensor by noon tomorrow? |
| 被動 | The sensor will have been checked by noon tomorrow. | The sensor will not have been checked by noon tomorrow. | Will the sensor have been checked by noon tomorrow? |

## 現在完成進行式

情境：從過去持續到現在，強調過程；一直檢查兩小時了。

主動公式：`have/has been + V-ing`；被動公式：`have/has been being + p.p.`。

| 語態 | 肯定 | 否定 | 疑問 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 主動 | The technician has been checking the sensor for two hours. | The technician has not been checking the sensor for two hours. | Has the technician been checking the sensor for two hours? |
| 被動（辨識用） | The sensor has been being checked for two hours. | The sensor has not been being checked for two hours. | Has the sensor been being checked for two hours? |

此被動在形式上可組合，但冗長且少見。實際表達優先使用本列主動句。若不重要的是持續過程，可改用簡單或完成被動，但語意重點會改變。

否定句中的 for two hours 可能否定「有在做」或「已持續兩小時」；若只想說尚不足兩小時，寫 `... but for less than two hours`；若完全未檢查，用 `has/had not checked` 並依參考時間調整。不要把長度否定當成唯一固定中文意思。

## 替換人稱，不要全句亂改

`I check → I do not check → Do I check?`；`She checks → She does not check → Does she check?`
`I am checking → We are checking → He is checking`。
`I have checked → She has checked → They have checked`。
`She checked → They checked`：一般過去式除 be 外不隨人稱改變。
`She will check → They will check`：will 後都用原形。

## 一個動作延伸到疑問詞

- What does she check? 她檢查什麼？what 取代受詞。
- Who checks the sensor? 誰檢查感測器？who 本身作主詞，一般肯定主詞問句不加 does。
- When did she check it? 她何時檢查？did 後回原形。
- How long has she been checking it? 她已經檢查多久？問持續長度。
- Why was it checked again? 為何又檢查了一次？被動的疑問。
- Hasn't she checked it yet? 她還沒檢查嗎？否定疑問常帶期待或驚訝，不是中性的 Has she ...?

## 同一消息，用意不同

`She checked it.`：過去做過此事。
`She has checked it.`：已做過，對現在狀態有關。
`She was checking it when I called.`：我打電話當時正在做，不保證完成。
`She had checked it when I called.`：我打電話前已完成。
`She has been checking it.`：強調最近持續的過程，可能剛停或仍在做。

## 不適合硬套的組合

- 狀態動詞 know、belong、own 在一般認知／所有權意思通常不用進行式。`I know the answer.`
- 同一動詞換意思可用進行式：`I think it is safe.`（認為）vs. `I am thinking about the proposal.`（正在思考）。
- `I have a car.` 表所有權；`I am having lunch.` 表正在吃午餐。不是 have 永遠不能進行。
- 不及物 arrive、happen 通常不造被動；`The incident happened yesterday.`
- 每個句子都能加否定，不代表否定後語意仍適合同一情境。先決定要說的事，再選形式。

## 自己做六格練習

把 check the sensor 換成 inspect the pump、test the sample、review the report，各寫：現在習慣、昨天完成、現在正在、到現在已完成、明天會做、昨天被做。再各改一個否定及疑問。答案不能只看尾巴；要檢查主詞、助動詞與動詞原形／分詞是否一起改對。
