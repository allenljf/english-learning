# 介系詞與搭配：用關係圖記，不逐字翻譯

## 時間：點、日、段與截止

| 搭配 | 心中畫面 | 例句與意思 | 常見陷阱 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| at 9 a.m. / at noon / at night | 時間點 | The meeting starts at nine. 會議九點開始。 | night 慣用 at；一般 morning 用 in |
| on Monday / on May 3 / on Monday morning | 某一天的格子 | It arrives on Friday morning. 週五上午到。 | 加了特定日期的 morning 用 on |
| in October / in 2026 / in the morning | 一段較大的範圍 | We start in October. 十月開始。 | next Monday 通常不加 on |
| by Friday | 最晚的截止線 | Submit it by Friday. 最晚週五提交。 | 不表示整段時間持續做 |
| until Friday | 持續到截止線 | The office is closed until Friday. 辦公室持續關閉到週五。 | 不等於「最晚交件」 |
| within three days | 一個期限範圍內 | Reply within three days. 三天內回覆。 | 注意從哪天起算 |
| in three days | 從現在推移三天 | The parts will arrive in three days. 零件三天後到。 | 不等於三天之內任何時候 |
| for three days | 持續長度 | We tested it for three days. 我們測了三天。 | for 不只用於完成式 |
| since Monday | 起始點到參考時間 | It has been offline since Monday. 自週一起離線。 | since 後不能直接放 three days 表長度 |
| during the test | 某事件期間 | Do not open it during the test. 測試期間勿開。 | 後面接名詞，不是完整句 |
| while we test it | 兩個事件同時 | Wait while we test it. 我們測試時請等候。 | while 是連接詞，後可接子句 |
| throughout the day | 整段時間 | Staff are available throughout the day. 全天有人可協助。 | 比 during 更強調全程 |
| from Monday to Friday | 起點與終點 | Training runs from Monday to Friday. 訓練週一到週五進行。 | from 與 to 成對記 |
| between 9 and 11 | 兩個界線 | Call between 9 and 11. 九點到十一點間來電。 | between ... and，不配 to |
| before / after lunch | 事件先後 | Call after lunch. 午餐後打電話。 | 後接 V-ing：after checking |

`not until`：`We will not restart until the inspection is complete.` 檢查完成才重啟。不要把 not 漏掉誤讀成「完成前就重啟」。

`on time`＝準時，`in time`＝來得及。`The train arrived on time.` 火車準點。`We arrived in time for the meeting.` 我們趕上會議，可能提早到。

## 地點與方向

| 對比 | 例句 | 記法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at / in | Meet at the entrance. 在入口見；Work in the cleanroom. 在無塵室內工作。 | at 看作點；in 看作內部空間 |
| on / above / over | The label is on the box. 標籤貼箱子上；A lamp is above the bench. 燈在工作台上方。 | on 通常接觸；above 強調較高；over 可表跨越、覆蓋等 |
| under / below | The cable is under the desk. 線在桌下；Output is below target. 產出低於目標。 | under 常在正下方或受約束；below 可表數值 |
| to / into | Go to the lab. 去實驗室；Put it into the box. 放入箱內。 | to 是目的地；into 是從外進內 |
| in / into | It is in the box. 已在裡面；Put it into the box. 進入的動作。 | 靜態位置與方向不同 |
| across / through | Walk across the yard. 穿越院子；Walk through the corridor. 穿過走廊。 | across 跨過平面；through 穿行空間內 |
| beside / besides | Sit beside me. 坐我旁邊；Besides gloves, wear goggles. 除手套還要護目鏡。 | beside 旁邊；besides 加上 |
| between / among | between the two machines；among the employees | between 可指清楚分別的多方，不只限兩個；among 看作群體 |
| near / next to | near the office；next to the printer | near 靠近；next to 緊鄰 |

## 常見動詞＋介系詞

| 搭配 | 原創例句與中文 |
| --- | --- |
| apply for + 職缺 | She applied for the position. 她申請該職缺。 |
| apply to + 公司／適用對象 | This rule applies to all visitors. 這規則適用所有訪客。 |
| participate in / take part in | We participated in training. 我們參加訓練。 |
| attend + 活動（無介系詞） | We attended the workshop. 我們出席工作坊。 |
| comply with | Suppliers must comply with the policy. 供應商須遵守政策。 |
| depend on / rely on | We depend on accurate data. 我們仰賴正確資料。 |
| respond to / reply to | Please reply to this email. 請回覆此信。 |
| discuss + 問題（無 about） | We discussed the issue. 我們討論問題。 |
| explain A to B | Explain the process to the trainee. 向新人解釋製程。 |
| remind A of B | This alert reminds us of the risk. 警報提醒我們風險。 |
| remind A to V | Remind me to submit it. 提醒我提交。 |
| inform / notify A of B | Notify us of any changes. 有變更請告知我們。 |
| apologize to A for B | We apologized to the client for the delay. 我們因延誤向客戶道歉。 |
| thank A for B | Thank you for your help. 謝謝協助。 |
| provide A with B | We provide staff with gloves. 我們提供員工手套。 |
| provide B for A | We provide gloves for staff. 我們提供手套給員工。 |
| supply A with B | They supply us with parts. 他們供應我們零件。 |
| prevent A from V-ing | The cover prevents dust from entering. 蓋子阻止灰塵進入。 |
| protect A from / against B | Gloves protect hands from chemicals. 手套保護雙手免受化學品傷害。 |
| spend 時間／金錢 on N | We spent money on training. 我們花錢訓練。 |
| spend 時間 V-ing | We spent an hour checking it. 我們花一小時檢查。 |
| invest in | We invested in new tools. 我們投資新工具。 |
| pay for | The company pays for travel. 公司支付差旅費。 |
| charge A for B | They charged us for delivery. 他們向我們收運費。 |
| benefit from | Staff benefit from the training. 員工受益於訓練。 |
| refer to | Refer to the manual. 參閱手冊。 |
| deal with | She deals with complaints. 她處理客訴。 |
| account for | Transport accounts for 10% of the cost. 運輸占成本百分之十。 |
| consist of | The kit consists of three tools. 套組由三個工具組成。 |
| result in | The fault resulted in a shutdown. 故障造成停機。 |
| result from | The shutdown resulted from a fault. 停機源於故障。 |
| lead to | This change may lead to delays. 此變更可能導致延誤。 |
| contribute to | Training contributes to safety. 訓練有助安全。 |
| belong to | This tool belongs to our team. 工具屬於我們團隊。 |
| dispose of | Dispose of waste properly. 妥善處置廢棄物。 |
| object to + N / V-ing | They objected to changing the schedule. 他們反對改時程。 |
| look forward to + N / V-ing | I look forward to hearing from you. 期待收到回覆。 |

記憶重點：每次背「動詞＋洞位」，例如 provide「誰」with「什麼」，而不是只記 provide＝提供。

## 關係相同，不代表用字相同

`enter the room` 不用 into，但 `go into the room` 可以。
`contact the vendor` 不用 with，但 `communicate with the vendor` 需要 with。
`reach the factory` 不用 at，但 `arrive at the factory` 需要 at。
`answer the question` 不用 to，但 `respond to the question` 需要 to。
`marry someone` 不用 with；`be married to someone` 使用 to。

介系詞後面通常接名詞、代名詞受格或 V-ing：`with him`、`without checking`。`to` 可能是不定詞標記也可能是介系詞，要連同前面片語判斷。

## 新手的四格練習

拿 `responsible for` 做四格：

1. 主詞：`The technician is responsible for maintenance.` 技術員負責維護。
2. 時間：`The technician was responsible for maintenance.` 技術員當時負責維護。
3. 否定：`The technician is not responsible for purchasing.` 技術員不負責採購。
4. V-ing：`The technician is responsible for checking the sensors.` 技術員負責檢查感測器。

只變其中一格，先維持其他條件不動。能解釋差異後才打散混合。
